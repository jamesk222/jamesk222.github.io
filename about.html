About

Data engineer turned analyst, analyst turned business translator.

I didn't start in BI. I started underneath it — building the APIs and databases that dashboards quietly depend on. That backend grounding is why I now build reporting that's not just pretty, but correct, fast, and trusted by the people making decisions off it.

The short version

I'm a Business Intelligence & Data Analyst based in Pakistan with over five years of combined experience across data analytics, BI dashboard development, statistical modelling and backend data engineering. I hold an MSc in International Business with Data Analytics from the University of Ulster, UK, and a BSc in Computer Systems Engineering from UET, Pakistan.

My work sits at the intersection of three things most analysts treat separately: the pipeline (SQL, Azure Data Factory, Microsoft Fabric), the dashboard (Power BI, Tableau, DAX), and the narrative (GenAI-assisted reporting that explains the "so what" to stakeholders who don't read raw tables). That combination is what lets me ship analytics that get used, not just published.

How I got here

I began as a back-end developer building API-driven data pipelines and relational schemas — work that taught me how data actually breaks before I ever built a chart on top of it. From there I moved into dedicated data analyst and BI analyst roles, running root-cause analysis, ETL automation and executive reporting for a full-service company, then into remote contract analytics work for multiple clients, where I built fraud/churn detection models, demand forecasting pipelines and AI-assisted reporting workflows.

Along the way I picked up a postgraduate qualification specifically to close the gap between "can build the model" and "can explain why it matters to a business" — which is now the core of how I position myself: BI and analytics work that's judged on business outcomes, not just technical correctness.

What I'm looking for

I'm targeting hybrid roles that combine BI, marketing analytics, digital analytics and AI automation — ideally at a multinational organisation. I'm comfortable remote, open to hybrid or on-site arrangements, and open to relocation for the right opportunity.
